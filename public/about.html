About Chama

Chama is inspired by historical traditions of cooperative communal support, such as Esusu, Ajo, and rotating savings groups. We leverage modern web architecture to bring transparency, speed, and seamless peer-to-peer coordination to members globally.

Our Core Vision

To build a trusted ecosystem where individuals can pool mutual resources, support community growth, and earn rotational returns while fostering leadership mentorship and financial cooperation.
